## 2024-05-24 - Accessibility and Interaction Improvements in Mixer
**Learning:** Adding `aria-label` to range inputs in complex components like the Mixer is crucial for screen reader users to understand context (Deck/Type). Double-click to reset is a standard pattern in DJ software that enhances usability.
**Action:** Always include specific `aria-label` attributes for inputs that rely on visual context, and consider power-user interactions like double-click resets for sliders/knobs.
